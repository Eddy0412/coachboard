Last week we showed you the new Film Room. This week: CoachIQ, our AI scouting tool that reads the plays you've already tagged and turns them into a real scouting report — no extra data entry.

Built from your own film

Once a project has 20+ tagged plays, CoachIQ streams a scouting report right below the video player:

  • A per-down action breakdown showing tendencies by down and distance.
  • A pass-target heatmap built from your tagged plays.
  • Reports save automatically with a last-analyzed timestamp, so you always know how current it is.
Share it your way

A visibility toggle lets you keep a report to yourself and your staff, or share it out. When you're ready, export a branded PDF — with the Coachboard watermark and attribution — to print or send ahead of gameday.
