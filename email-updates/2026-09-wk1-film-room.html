We've been shipping updates to make watching and breaking down film feel less like software and more like being in the room. Here's what's new this week.

A redesigned Film Room

The Film Room workspace has been rebuilt around watching and tagging film without breaking flow:

  • A floating timestamps panel keeps your coaching points visible while the video stays front and center.
  • Autoplay steps through your tagged plays automatically, with a speed control and a highlighted active timestamp so you always know where you are.
  • Prev/next playlist navigation lets you jump play-to-play in one tap — great for reviewing a specific package or a single player's reps.
Built for coaching from your phone

The whole mobile experience got a rework. Navigation now tucks off-canvas so video gets the full screen, and Film Room on mobile has its own stacked view designed for one-handed reviewing between reps — on the sideline, on the bus, wherever you're coaching from.

Coming in the next few weeks
