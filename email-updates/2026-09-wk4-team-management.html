Last week we showed you the Knowledge Base. This week: new controls for the moments a team's roster changes — a coach moves on, a head coach hands off the reins, or a season wraps and a team needs to go away.

Leave a team, or hand off head coach

Any coach can leave a team on their own — the roster and your teammates are unaffected. If you're the head coach, Coachboard walks you through handing the role to another coach on staff before you go, so a team is never left without one.

Archive or delete, your call

If you're the last one on a team, you decide what happens to it: archive it and keep the roster and film saved and viewable anytime, or delete it to clear it from your team list entirely.

Where to find it

Open a team's page and look for Leave Team. Already archived a team? It's still there under Archived Teams whenever you need to look back.
